Words used in this manual:

- **The bubble**: the Private Space Bubble creates. Its apps appear in the *Private* section of the app drawer.
- **The normal phone**: your phone outside the bubble (Android's main user).
- **The Meta space**: an optional, separate work profile for apps that need Google to start (Facebook, Instagram). Its apps appear in the *Work* tab.
- **The engine**: the part of Bubble that runs with Android's `shell` identity, through wireless debugging, for tasks that need system permissions.
- **The guard**: a small engine process that keeps Google services out and re-applies the phone's firewall.
