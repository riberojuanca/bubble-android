# 7. Bubble Apps, the widget and shortcuts

Android hides the bubble's apps inside the *Private* section and the Meta space's apps inside the *Work* tab. Bubble gives you two faster ways to reach them.

## Bubble Apps

**Bubble Apps** is a second icon of Bubble in your app drawer (the bubble logo with four lilac dots). It is not a separate app: it comes with Bubble, belongs to it and goes away with it. You can also open it from Bubble's home screen (**Bubble Apps** row).

It shows a grid with two sections:

- **Meta space**: its apps, with the work badge. They open at once.
- **Bubble**: the bubble's apps. They are the list Bubble last read, shown without waiting; pull down to read it again.

Tap an app to open it. If the bubble is locked, Android asks you to unlock it first; if you cancel, nothing opens.

**Press and hold** an app to add its icon to your home screen; Android asks you to confirm.

The icon is a one-cell widget, not a shortcut: the launcher puts the icon of the app that made a shortcut on its corner (Bubble's, in a circle) and only system apps can change that. The widget shows the app's real icon with its name; bubble apps carry Bubble's small mark, which follows the light or dark theme, and Meta apps carry the work badge. Icons are added one at a time.

Shortcuts made by older versions still work. Bubble redraws them with the app's real icon each time it starts, but the launcher keeps its own mark on them: remove them and add the icon again to get rid of it. On launchers that do not accept widgets from apps, Bubble adds a shortcut instead.

The button at the bottom, **Add the widget to the home screen**, places the widget (below).

## The widget

Android does not let apps create folders on the home screen, so Bubble offers a widget that works like one: Bubble's logo and your apps, Meta first (with the work badge), then the bubble's.

- **Add it**: Bubble Apps → **Add the widget to the home screen**, and confirm. If your launcher does not allow it, press and hold an empty spot of the home screen → **Widgets** → **Bubble**.
- **Size**: 4×2 by default. Two rows of icons fill it exactly; the icons follow your launcher's icon size (a little smaller, as they have no names). Resize it by pressing and holding it.
- **More apps than fit**: swipe up and down inside the widget. The edge with more apps behind it fades out.
- **Light and dark**: the widget is almost transparent, and its logo follows the system theme.
- **Updates**: it redraws when apps are added or removed, when Bubble reads the bubble's apps and every 30 minutes. If it looks out of date, open Bubble Apps and pull down.

## Opening a bubble app from the widget or a shortcut

A small screen appears while Bubble opens the app:

- The **logo** at the top and a **loading bar** in the middle, with what Bubble is doing.
- If the bubble is locked, a **padlock** at the bottom. Android asks for your fingerprint or PIN; when you give it, the padlock opens and the app appears. If you cancel, the screen closes.
- If the bubble is already open, the app opens directly.

This works without Wi-Fi: Bubble asks its copy inside the bubble to open the app.
