# 11. Deleting the bubble and removing everything

## Remove one app

Bubble → Apps (or Meta space) → the **bin** next to the app. Only that space's copy and its data are deleted.

## Delete the bubble

**Bubble → Bubble → Delete the bubble**, and confirm. The bubble and all its apps and data are deleted. This cannot be undone. Bubble then shows the setup assistant to create a new one, if you want.

**Delete and create a new bubble** does the same and creates a fresh bubble right away.

## Delete the Meta space

**Bubble → Meta space → Delete the Meta space**. Its apps and data are deleted; the bubble and the normal phone do not change.

## Remove everything

**Bubble → Settings → Remove everything** leaves the phone as it was before Bubble. Bubble first lists what goes and what stays, and asks twice, because apps and data are lost.

**Removed**
- The bubble, with its apps and data.
- The Meta space, with its apps and data.
- The phone's firewall: blocked apps get internet again.
- The settings Bubble changed in the system: the silenced system events, the usage-statistics restriction, Bubble's saved state; the guard is stopped.

**Kept**
- The apps and data of your normal phone.
- Bubble itself, installed and paired, so you can uninstall it or start again.

Then uninstall Bubble as any app. To remove the pairing too: **Settings → System → Developer options → Revoke debugging authorizations**. You can turn Developer options off afterwards.

> If you uninstall Bubble **without** using Remove everything first, the bubble and the Meta space stay on the phone. You can still delete them from Android's Settings: the bubble under Security & privacy → Private space → Delete private space; the Meta space under Settings → Passwords & accounts → Work → Remove work profile (names vary by phone).
